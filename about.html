About Night City Signal

An independent newsroom for people who live in the city, not above it.

Our mission

Night City runs on rumor, and rumor is usually wrong. We report what we can verify, show our sources when we safely can, and correct mistakes in public. We cover the corporations, but we spend more time in the neighborhoods those corporations forgot.

The Signal is funded by readers and a handful of small advertisers. We take no money from firms we cover.
