PUBLICATION CONCEPT
Night City Signal is a fictional independent news outlet set in the Cyberpunk RED tabletop universe (Night City, 2045).
It reports street-level stories, made by medias (The settings reporters). This is fan-made and unofficial, as Cyberpunk
RED belongs to R. Talsorian Games, not me.
